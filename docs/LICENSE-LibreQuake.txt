Reproduced verbatim from the LibreQuake project (docs/COPYING), because a tidied
licence is not the licence that was granted. That is the same rule
docs/LICENSE-Freedoom.txt is kept under.

THE TEXTURES ARE THE BETTER-EVIDENCED HALF. The note below enumerates the BSD-3
side as "models, textures and sounds" -- textures by NAME. The maps reach BSD-3
only as residue, which is spelled out further down. Same licence, two different
strengths of chain, and worth knowing which is which.

LibreQuake ships under two licences and says so in its own
docs/README-IMPORTANT-LICENCE-INFO:

    bsd3 is permissive and is what all the models textures and sounds are under
    gpl2 is more restrictive and just applies to the quakec, progs.dat and
    pop.lmp

READ THAT QUOTATION AGAIN: the word "maps" is not in it, on either side. The
maps are BSD-3 by RESIDUE, not by enumeration -- the licence granted over the
project is the BSD-3 above (their docs/COPYING, reproduced here verbatim), and
that sentence removes only the QuakeC, progs.dat and pop.lmp from it. Their
docs/CREDITS lists Maps among the contributions the project received, so the
maps are contributions to the project this licence is the licence of.

That is a sound chain and it is worth writing out, because an earlier version of
this file and of README.md paraphrased it as "BSD-3 for the maps, models,
textures and sounds" -- putting a word into a list that does not contain it. A
reader checking GitHub's licence badge for this repository is told NOASSERTION,
so the paraphrase was the only thing standing between that reader and the wrong
conclusion.

BSD-3 is the only side this game could have taken from: it bakes its maps INTO
the executable, so a copyleft map would be a copyleft binary. README.md rejected
OpenArena and Xonotic on exactly that reasoning, and the same test is what chose
this.

Nothing GPL-2 from LibreQuake is used, distributed, or linked here. No QuakeC,
no progs.dat, no pop.lmp.

한국어: LibreQuake 프로젝트의 docs/COPYING을 그대로 옮겼습니다. 정돈된 라이선스는
허락받은 그 라이선스가 아니기 때문이며, docs/LICENSE-Freedoom.txt와 같은 규칙입니다.
이곳에서 이 라이선스가 덮는 것은 assets/maps/lqdm1.map("Solstice")입니다. 위에 인용한
LibreQuake 자신의 문장을 다시 보십시오. "맵"이라는 낱말은 양쪽 어디에도 없습니다. 맵이
BSD-3인 것은 *열거*가 아니라 *잔여*에 의해서입니다. 프로젝트에 부여된 라이선스가 위의
BSD-3(그들의 docs/COPYING을 그대로 옮긴 것)이고, 그 문장은 거기에서 QuakeC와 progs.dat과
pop.lmp만을 덜어 냅니다. 그리고 그들의 docs/CREDITS는 프로젝트가 받은 기여에 Maps를
포함시킵니다. 이 파일과 README.md의 이전 판이 그것을 "맵·모델·텍스처·사운드는 BSD-3"으로
바꿔 말했기에 적어 둡니다. 그 목록에 없는 낱말을 넣은 것이었습니다.

이 게임은 맵을 실행 파일 *안에* 굽기 때문에 카피레프트 맵은 곧 카피레프트 바이너리이며, README.md가 OpenArena와 Xonotic을 거절한
